# Zayna — a first look

### An entertainment-management concept from Bryan

Zayna, I put together an early prototype to explore how a simple tool could help with event promotion, inquiries, and organization. This is a quick overview for your feedback—not an official event announcement or a live booking site.

## The idea

**One place to present an event, prepare promotional content, and keep inquiries organized.**

### A page designed for phones
A mobile-first landing page to introduce an event and its confirmed details. The prototype keeps ticket links disabled until they are verified.

### Easier inquiries
Consent-aware inquiry forms to demonstrate how questions could be collected. There is no working inquiry form on this overview page, and it does not collect contact information.

### Promotional drafts
Draft promotional wording for you to review and change before anything is sent or published. The prototype does not send messages or publish posts.

### Frequently asked questions
A local FAQ assistant in the prototype, intended to help visitors find answers. Event facts still need your confirmation; this overview does not provide verified booking information.

### A sample dashboard
A demonstration of sales and referral reporting using made-up records. The sample figures are not actual ticket sales or revenue.

## What this is—and is not

This is an early concept for discussion. The interactive prototype remains on Bryan's computer; **this link is a view-only overview, not access to that app**.

There are no payments, live ticket sales, customer messages, or booking commitments here. Dates, prices, venue details, organizer authority, and branding permissions have not been confirmed.
